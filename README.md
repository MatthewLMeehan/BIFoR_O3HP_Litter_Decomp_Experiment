# BIFoR_O3HP_Litter_Decomp_Experiment
Litter decomposition experiment performed at the field sites BIFoR FACE (Birmingham Institute of Forest Research Free-Air CO2 Enrichment, United Kingdom) and O3HP (Oak Observatory at the Observatoire de Haute-Provence, France) from 2020-2022.
